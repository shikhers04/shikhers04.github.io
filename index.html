Hello!

Welcome to my homepage. I am Shikher Sharma, currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Mathematics, Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.

Originally from Gola Gokarannath, I moved to Delhi to pursue my undergraduate and postgraduate studies at the University of Delhi, before continuing my research journey at Banaras Hindu University, where I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics.

My research focuses on optimization techniques on different kinds of spaces and their applications. I am particularly interested in developing efficient algorithms for large-scale problems and exploring their applications. My work includes iterative methods on Hadamard manifolds and Hilbert spaces.

Beyond academics, I enjoy running, cricket and badminton. I also enjoy travelling and exploring different countries, cultures and perspectives.
